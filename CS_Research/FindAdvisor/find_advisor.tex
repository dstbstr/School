I was looking at working with professor Chris Cain as an advisor.
He and I have known each other for a few years now, and he is part of the reason that I entered EWU.

Chris has written a couple of research papers that caught my attention. 
His PhD dissertation was focused on gamification in the learning environment.~\cite{cain_tingle_2018}
This is relevant to me both because my day job is working at Xbox, and because I am pursuing a graduate degree in part to consider becoming an educator.
An earlier paper explored the classroom gamification aspect, which was later used in his PhD dissertation.~\cite{cain_content-independent_2016}

The topic I've been exploring has been closer to the game side of the field, focusing on game balance and economies.
I believe that Dr. Cain and I would work well together in this space, as his expertise will complement my interests.
Realistically, I don't imagine that this work will require a co-advisor as this is a fairly specific area of research, and Dr. Cain's experience is well suited to continue research in this area.
