# The Ontology of Incremental Games
* "The defining feature of incremental games are numbers that increase at an incrementally increasing rate."
* Not unique to just incremental games, RPGs with experience are another example
* Incremental games are different because they strip away most other mechanics
* Many RPGs eventually hit the wall and require repetitive play called 'grinding'
* In a way, and incremental game is making the computer do the grinding for the player
* Another common feature in incremental games is parody or satire
